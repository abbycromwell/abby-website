# Abby Cromwell — Product Decisions

## Purpose

Create an employer-facing portfolio for Abby Cromwell. Its primary job is to encourage entertainment-industry employers to view Abby's work.

## Audience

The primary audience is employers and hiring teams in entertainment industries looking for an artist.

## Creative Practice

Abby's work includes illustrative art, character design, and storytelling. Game design is an area of interest to represent where relevant.

## Experience Goals

- Make the work feel central, memorable, and inviting to explore.
- Create curiosity and encourage interaction with the portfolio.
- Let the art communicate Abby's sensibility rather than relying on generic portfolio conventions.
- Give the About page a more restrained, simple pace as a break from the work-focused pages.

## Starting Site Map

1. **Home** — Introduce Abby Cromwell and present a concise overview of selected artwork and recent projects. The page will include a background 3D model designed by Abby.
2. **Work** — A curated selection of art rather than a complete archive.
3. **Case Study** — One case-study page remains in the initial site map. Its project and content are intentionally open and will be supplied later.
4. **About** — A visually simpler page with a short bio, email contact, and availability.
